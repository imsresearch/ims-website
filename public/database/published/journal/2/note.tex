\chapter*{Note from the Journal coordinator}\addcontentsline{toc}{chapter}{Note from the Journal coordinator}

During the past few months, our Department has gone through some major changes. The Institute elected Editor-in-chief matcha\_tea, who asked me to lead the project of adopting our Constitution. In an effort to professionalize and formalize our Department's work, we have established our policies and have written useful step-by-step guides for all roles throughout the writing, reviewing, and editing stages. We have also rebranded as the \textit{Institute of Minecraft Studies Publishing Department} to better reflect what we focus on.

I would like to personally thank everyone who has helped throughout this process. Going forward, we plan to publish a new issue of this Journal every three months. We ask anyone who is interested to come join us and help the Department thrive: whether it is through writing news articles, opinion pieces, or formal theories; performing research or experiments; or editing others' works. We are always welcoming new reporters, columnists, and editors into our Department, and we provide mentoring to new members so that they can get started more easily. Even a couple of hours a week can help us tremendously. Don't worry; you can change your mind anytime. If you would like to try this out, \link{https://imsresearch.pages.dev/pubdep/jobs}{give it a shot}!

\begin{flushright}
    \textit{---koviubi, Journal coordinator}
\end{flushright}
